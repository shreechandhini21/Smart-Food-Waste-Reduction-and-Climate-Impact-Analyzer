# Sources used for implementation assumptions

## Portion sizes
- USDA FoodData Central-derived references: medium idli around 40 g; medium plain dosa around 80 g; cooked rice around 158 g per cup.
- Chennai food-service listings: examples include 300 g pongal, 250 g upma and 405 g chicken biryani servings.
- These are **reference portions**, not mandatory standards. Restaurant serving size must be measured and can be edited in the project data.

## Food donation
FSSAI's surplus food regulations state that donated surplus must be safe, appropriately handled, and handed over before spoilage/expiry. The regulations also specify hygiene, storage/transport and record-keeping responsibilities.

## Waste and climate
EPA material documents that food waste can produce methane in landfills and that anaerobic digestion and composting can divert suitable organic material. The project therefore treats diversion as a potential avoided-disposal-impact pathway rather than claiming measured atmospheric CO2 removal.

## Important research limitation
The synthetic dataset is a controlled simulation. Emission factors in the project are illustrative and must be replaced with documented, locally applicable factors before publication.
